# Royal Courier Public Service Taxonomy

This document provides a high-level public taxonomy of Royal Courier Inc. service categories.

It is intentionally descriptive rather than operational. It does not expose internal dispatch rules, pricing logic, customer-specific procedures, margins, routing logic, or real-time capacity.

The Royal Courier website remains the canonical source for current commercial service information.

## Entity Scope

This taxonomy describes Royal Courier Inc.

Royal Courier Inc. and Royal Expediting Inc. are separate legal entities and should not be treated as the same organization.

## Core Service Categories

### Same-Day Courier

Time-sensitive point-to-point delivery for commercial shipments requiring pickup and delivery on the same day.

### Expedited Freight

Urgent commercial freight movement where standard shipping timelines are not sufficient.

### Dedicated / Exclusive-Use Transportation

A vehicle is assigned to a shipment for dedicated movement rather than shared routing.

### Straight-Truck Service

Commercial transportation using straight trucks when shipment size, weight, access, or equipment needs exceed ordinary courier-vehicle requirements.

### Liftgate Service

Vehicle-based liftgate support for locations where loading-dock access is unavailable or unsuitable.

### Pallet-Jack Service

Pallet-jack support for applicable palletized pickups and deliveries.

### Inside Pickup and Delivery

Pickup or delivery that requires movement beyond the exterior loading point, subject to shipment and site requirements.

### Commercial White-Glove Transportation

Higher-touch commercial handling and delivery for shipments requiring more care, coordination, or controlled placement than standard curbside freight movement.

### Medical Courier

Time-sensitive courier transportation for eligible medical and healthcare-related materials within Royal Courier's supported service scope.

### O'Hare Cargo Recovery

Recovery and onward delivery of eligible commercial cargo from O'Hare-related cargo facilities and handling locations.

### Failed-Delivery Recovery

Recovery of shipments that could not be completed through the original delivery path and require alternate pickup, redelivery, or rescue coordination.

### Show Freight

Time-sensitive transportation supporting trade shows, exhibitions, and related commercial events.

### Production-Down / Technician Parts Delivery

Urgent transportation of parts or materials needed to support technicians, maintenance teams, or production continuity.

### Construction Material Delivery

Commercial delivery of eligible construction materials, tools, parts, and jobsite-related shipments within supported service conditions.

## Service Relationships

These categories can overlap. A single shipment may require more than one service attribute.

For example:

- expedited freight + dedicated use
- straight truck + liftgate + pallet jack
- O'Hare cargo recovery + same-day delivery
- failed-delivery recovery + inside delivery
- production-down parts + dedicated use
- construction material delivery + liftgate

These relationships are descriptive and do not replace shipment-specific dispatch review.

## Service Boundaries

Service eligibility depends on factors such as pickup and delivery location, shipment dimensions and weight, timing, access, equipment requirements, handling requirements, and site conditions.

This taxonomy does not imply capabilities, certifications, or regulatory permissions that Royal Courier has not explicitly documented.
